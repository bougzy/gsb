# 1. UNDERSTANDING YOUR PRODUCT VALUE

## What Problem Does GSAMS Solve?

### The Pain Points (Use These in Your Messages)

**For Schools:**
- Teachers waste 15-30 minutes per class on attendance
- Students sign for absent friends (proxy attendance)
- Parents complain they don't know if children attended
- No real-time visibility into attendance patterns
- Manual registers get lost or damaged
- Reports take hours to compile
- Exam eligibility disputes due to poor records

**For Churches:**
- Don't know actual membership attendance
- Can't track who's been absent for weeks
- Tithing records disconnected from attendance
- No way to follow up on missing members
- Special events have no proper headcount
- Volunteer scheduling is chaotic

**For Training Companies:**
- Compliance audits require attendance proof
- Can't verify participants actually attended
- Certificate fraud (people claim they attended but didn't)
- Manual tracking doesn't scale
- No way to prove training happened

**For Corporate HR:**
- Remote workers claim false hours
- No verification of field staff locations
- Time theft costs money
- Compliance issues with labor laws
- Manual timesheets are unreliable
